# Original texts for weaving

English texts that are free to use, kept untouched, as the starting point for woven versions. How an original is added: "Adding a story to this repository" in [`../README.md`](../README.md).

## `grimm-wolf-and-seven-kids.txt`

- **Text:** The Wolf and the Seven Little Kids, from *Household Tales* by Jacob and Wilhelm Grimm, translated by Margaret Hunt (1884); 1,112 words.
- **Source:** the Standard Ebooks edition, <https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/jacob-grimm_wilhelm-grimm/household-tales/margaret-hunt>, fetched 2026-11-21.
- **Copyright:** the Grimms died in 1859 and 1863 and Hunt in 1912, so the text is out of copyright in the EU and public domain in the United States. Standard Ebooks releases its own editing into the public domain.
- **Changes:** none, apart from removing the markup; "hardly had she make one cut" is in the source.
