# Candidate texts for weaving

English texts that could be added to this folder, with how far the copyright status of each was checked. What is already here is in [`README.md`](README.md).

Checked on 2026-11-21 by web search. "Confirmed" means the dates were seen in a source that day; anything else is said to be from memory. Sizes are rough and from memory. Check the status again, for your own country, before adding a text.

## What makes a good text

- **Free to use:** the author and, for a translation, the translator dead for more than seventy years (the rule in the EU), and public domain in the United States; or a free licence. A modern retelling or a new translation of an old story has its own copyright.
- **Plain English,** not too old-fashioned.
- **A lot of dialogue and everyday content:** family, eating, houses, going somewhere. The common words of a language come up most there.
- **A size that fits:** a few hundred to a few thousand words for a story woven in several versions; a whole book only for reading through at one level.

## Where to get clean files

- **Standard Ebooks,** <https://standardebooks.org>: carefully edited editions, its own editing released into the public domain. The Hunt translation of Grimm is at <https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/jacob-grimm_wilhelm-grimm/household-tales/margaret-hunt>.
- **Project Gutenberg,** <https://www.gutenberg.org>: Grimm (Hunt) is book 5314, Oz 55 and 43936, Aesop (Townsend) 21, Aesop (Jacobs) 28, Peter Rabbit 14304, Fansler 8299. Gutenberg asks that its name and header be removed from changed copies, and reminds readers to check the law of their own country.
