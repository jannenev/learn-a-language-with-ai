# Woven stories for Cebuano

English stories with Cebuano core words woven in: a second way of reading, beside the numbered texts in [`../texts/`](../texts/). What a woven story is and how to read one: [`weaving/README.md`](../../weaving/README.md).

The weaving was done with AI assistance and has not been checked by a native speaker.

## The stories

Read the versions of a story in order, some days apart.

| Story                                                                         | Reading | File                                                                 | Cebuano words | Share of the story |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| The Wolf and the Seven Little Kids (Grimm, translated by Margaret Hunt, 1884) | first   | [`wolf-and-seven-kids.12-words.md`](wolf-and-seven-kids.12-words.md) | 12            | about one word in eight |
|                                                                               | second  | [`wolf-and-seven-kids.21-words.md`](wolf-and-seven-kids.21-words.md) | 21            | a little under one in five |
|                                                                               | third   | [`wolf-and-seven-kids.31-words.md`](wolf-and-seven-kids.31-words.md) | 31            | between one in five and one in four |
| Little Redcap (Grimm, translated by Margaret Hunt, 1884)                      | first   | [`little-redcap.12-words.md`](little-redcap.12-words.md)             | 12            | about one in six or seven |
|                                                                               | second  | [`little-redcap.21-words.md`](little-redcap.21-words.md)             | 21            | about one in five |
|                                                                               | third   | [`little-redcap.30-words.md`](little-redcap.30-words.md)             | 30            | about one in four |

The words woven in are core words from [`../vocabulary.md`](../vocabulary.md), nearly all from Tier 1 and Tier 2, chosen by how common they are. The two stories were woven at different times and do not bring the words in quite the same order, so either can be read first; a reader who has not started the numbered texts can read these as well.
