# Word sets: the small words of Stage A, side by side

The texts bring the words in one at a time. This sheet shows the sets they belong to, so that a word met alone can be seen next to its neighbours: I next to you, here next to there, will-eat next to ate. Glance at it when a small word will not stick; the texts remain where the words are learned. Each set ends with a row to say aloud. Words in parentheses have not appeared in texts 001 to 003 yet. Written with AI assistance; not checked by a native speaker, the forms in parentheses least of all.

## 1. Persons

| | full form | short form, after the first word | mine, yours… |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | ako | ko | akoa |
| you, one person | ikaw | ka | imoha |
| he, she | siya | – | (iyaha) |
| we, you included | kita | ta | (atoa) |
| we, you not included | kami | mi | (amoa) |
| you, several | kamo | mo | (inyoha) |
| they | (sila) | – | (ilaha) |

Full form on its own or first (*Ako pud. Ikaw, Ma?*), short form after the first word (*Gusto ko. Nikaon na mi.*). Two we's: *ta* takes the listener in (*Kaon ta!*), *mi* leaves them out (*Maayo lang mi*). Say: *ako, ikaw, siya; kita, kami, kamo, sila*, and *ko, ka; ta, mi, mo*.

## 2. Pointing: three distances

| | near me | near you | away from both |
|---|---|---|---|
| this, that | kini, short *ni* | (kana, short *na*) | (kadto, short *to*) |
| here, there | diri, dinhi | (diha) | didto |
| come here, go there | (ari) | (anha) | adto |

Like Finnish *tämä, se, tuo*: *kini* = tämä, *kana* = se, *kadto* = tuo. The rows rhyme down the columns: *-i-* near me, *-a-* near you, *-to* far away. No plural forms; *mga* does that. *Adto* is the far member of the go-row: *Adto ta didto!* Say: *kini, kana, kadto; diri, diha, didto; ari, anha, adto*.

## 3. One verb, its shapes

| shape | says | kaon, eat | adto, go | tulog, sleep | inom, drink | hulat, wait |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| the root alone | do it! With *ta*: let's | Kaon! Kaon ta! | Adto ta! | Tulog na! | (Inom ta!) | Hulat lang! Hulat ta! |
| mo-, ma- | not yet happened: will, going to | mokaon | moadto | matulog | moinom | mohulat |
| ni-, na- | happened: did | nikaon | (niadto) | natulog | (niinom) | (nihulat) |
| nag- | is doing, was doing | (nagkaon) | – | – | (naginom) | (naghulat) |
| gi- | done to it; the thing is the subject | (gikaon) | – | – | (giinom) | (gihulat) |
| pag- | the act of; the thing | pagkaon, food | – | (pagtulog) | (pag-inom) | (paghulat) |

Not tense but aspect: whether a thing has happened, not when; *karon, unya, ugma* say when. *mo-* and *ni-* go with actions, *ma-* and *na-* with states (sleep, fall, get hurt). *Mokaon ko* is both "syön huomenna" and "aion syödä", *nikaon ko* both "söin" and "olen syönyt". Say one root down the column: *kaon, kaon ta, mokaon, nikaon, nagkaon, gikaon, pagkaon*.

## 4. When

| | |
|---|---|
| now, today | karon |
| later today | unya |
| tomorrow | ugma |
| yesterday | (gahapon) |
| morning, afternoon, evening and night | buntag, hapon, gabii: *maayong buntag, gabii na* |
| already, still | na, pa, in the slot |

The time word sits at the end and the verb does not change for it: *Moadto ba ka unya? Moadto ka sa trabaho ugma?* Say: *karon, unya, ugma; buntag, hapon, gabii*.
## 5. The slot after the first word

First word, then *na / pa / ba / lang / gyud / pud*, then *ko / ka / ta / mi / mo*. Every chain in the texts has this shape: *Adto na ta. Gusto ba mo? Naa pa ko'y trabaho. Naa ra ko diri.*

| particle | says | in the texts |
|---|---|---|
| na | already, now, anymore: the situation has changed | Nikaon **na** ko. Adto **na** ta. Dili **na**. |
| pa | still, yet, more: not changed yet | Naa **pa** ko'y trabaho. Wala **pa**. Gusto **pa** ko. |
| ba | makes a yes/no question | Gusto **ba** mo? Moadto **ba** ka unya? |
| lang, ra | just, only | Hulat **lang** diri. Naa **ra** ko diri. |
| gyud | really | Daghan **gyud**. Lami **gyud**! |
| pud | also, too | Ako **pud**. Maayo **pud**. |
| kaayo | very; follows its word, outside the slot | Daghan **kaayo**. Lami **kaayo**! |

Hear *na* and *pa* as a pair, already and still: *Nikaon na ko. Wala pa. Dili na. Naa pa.* Say: *na, pa, ba; lang, gyud, pud*.

## 6. The markers, in pairs

| job | before a thing | before a person's name |
|---|---|---|
| the one the sentence is about | ang: *ang pagkaon, ang mga tawo* | si: *si Mama, si Ana* |
| of, at, to, by | sa: *sa balay, sa trabaho* | ni: *trabaho ni Mama, pagkaon ni Ana* |
| a, some: what is wanted, eaten, bought | og: *og tubig, og pagkaon* | – |
| and | (ug): *pagkaon ug tubig* | (ug): *si Ana ug si Jun* |
| several | mga: *mga tawo* | – |
| joins a describing word to its noun | nga, -ng: *maayong buntag* | – |

*ang / si* and *sa / ni* are the same two jobs, for things and for names. *og* and *ug* sound the same; after a verb or *gusto* it is "some", between two things "and". Later: *kang* (to a person) and *nako, nimo*, which stand where *ni* + name stands. Say: *ang, si; sa, ni; og, ug, mga*.

## 7. No, and its yeses

| | the no | the yes |
|---|---|---|
| is not there, have not | wala, wala'y: *Wala'y tubig. Wala ko'y kwarta.* | naa, naa'y: *Naa'y tubig. Naa ko'y kwarta.* |
| did not, not yet | wala, wala pa: *Wala pa.* (*Wala ko nikaon.*) | na: *Nikaon na ko.* |
| not, will not; the plain no | dili: *Dili. Dili karon.* (*Dili ko moadto.*) | oo: *Oo.* |
| don't | ayaw: *Ayaw, Dong. Ayaw na.* | sige, or the bare root: *Sige. Kaon!* |

*Wala*: not there, did not happen. *Dili*: not so, will not be. Say: *dili, wala, ayaw*.
