# Learn a language with AI: Cebuano (Bisaya) reading texts

Short dialogues in Cebuano, the language of Cebu in the Philippines, called Bisaya by its speakers. They are built from a small core vocabulary, so the same words keep coming back and stick without memorising. Each text has the Cebuano on the left and the English on the right. The English is a word-for-word translation, so you can see which word is which. It is not meant to read as natural English.

**How to use a page.** Cover the right column and read the left. Uncover to check. Below the dialogue, *Word for word* gives, once per text, the words whose meaning is not visible in the English.

**How often.** Read the texts in order, and each text about six times over a month: twice on the first day, then after one, three, seven, fourteen and about thirty days, always the left column first. A word is yours when you read it cold without hesitating.

**Status.** These texts were written with the help of an AI assistant and have not yet been checked by a native speaker. Meanings are probably right; stress and naturalness are where errors would hide.
