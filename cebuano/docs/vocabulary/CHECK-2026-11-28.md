# The check of 2026-11-28: the tiers against real text and against spoken English

The word list of `cebuano/vocabulary.md` was made from knowledge of the language and had never been measured. This is the first measurement: the tiers counted in three kinds of ordinary Cebuano text, and matched against the 300 commonest words of spoken English.

**The tiers meant here are those of version 1 of the list,** as it stood on the day. Version 2 followed from this check; the count after it is in the last section.

**Status.** Done with AI assistance. The matching of English words to Cebuano ones is a first pass and is not checked by a native speaker. The counting is rough (see Limits). Nothing here is a decision; what was decided is in the [`README.md`](README.md) of this folder.

## The sources

| Name    | What it is                                                                      | Size                                      | Kind of language |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| tatoeba | The Cebuano sentences of Tatoeba, single sentences written by volunteers        | 1,893 sentences, 10,909 words             | Everyday, close to speech; small, and uneven: 55 of its sentences have the word *modagan*, runs |
| news    | The pages of the SunStar newspaper inside the Leipzig file `ceb_community_2017` | 478 pages, 8,752 sentences, 178,690 words | News of today, with readers' letters to an advice column |
| bible   | A Bible translation, from the OPUS collection `bible-uedin`                     | 35,313 lines, 864,616 words               | Old and formal (*gayud*, *daku*, *Jehova*); which translation was not found out |

Where they were fetched, on 2026-11-28:

- tatoeba: `https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/ceb/ceb_sentences.tsv.bz2`, third column.
- news: `https://downloads.wortschatz-leipzig.de/corpora/ceb_community_2017.tar.gz` (103 MB), then `python3 tools/wordfreq.py --leipzig-part sunstar.com.ph FOLDER > news.txt`.
- bible: `https://object.pouta.csc.fi/OPUS-bible-uedin/v1/mono/ceb.txt.gz`.

The texts are not in this repository: they are large and belong to others. The counts are.

Looked at and not used:

| Source                                  | Why not |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The Leipzig file as a whole             | 701,547 of its 702,495 pages are Cebuano Wikipedia, nearly all short articles of one pattern on a species. Its commonest words are *kaliwatan* (species) and *gihulagway* (described). See `weaving/WORD-LISTS.md` |
| OPUS `QED`, labelled Cebuano subtitles  | The file is English and Turkish |
| OPUS `CCMatrix`, Cebuano                | Sampled: Wikipedia articles on places, Bible verses and machine-translated shop pages mixed |

The Leipzig website answers a program with a check for robots, so the list of its other Cebuano files was not seen; the download address above works directly. A person with a browser can look at `https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download/Cebuano` for a news or web file without Wikipedia.

## How it was counted

```
python3 tools/wordfreq.py --out cebuano/docs/vocabulary/data tatoeba=tatoeba.txt news=news.txt bible=bible.txt
```

Counting is by entry, as in the course: *mokaon, nikaon, gikaon* count for *kaon*. Figures are occurrences per 10,000 running words. The full table is [`data/tier-counts.tsv`](data/tier-counts.tsv).

Tier 3 words by the same measure: *uban* (with, together: 6,393), *hunahuna* (think, idea, mind: 6,290), *gawas* (out: 2,983), *sakto, husto* (right: 2,627), *kinahanglan* (need, should, must: 2,459), *tuig* (year: 2,184), *sulti* (talk, speak: 2,125), *bisan* (any, anything, whatever: 2,085), *tinuod* (actually, fact, real: 2,034), *lain* (other, different, another, else: about 3,700).

What this match cannot be trusted for:

- **It follows English.** "Get" and "take" are places 18 and 72 in spoken English, and *kuha* has 1.8, 7.3 and 15 per 10,000 in the three Cebuano sources. "Think" is place 28; Cebuano often says it another way (*murag*, *siguro*, *abi nako*).
- **Several of its candidates hardly occur in the Cebuano sources:** *basin* 0.9 and 1.2 per 10,000 in tatoeba and news; *gamit* 0.9 and 1.1; *sulay, kaysa, sigurado, siyempre* about 0. Either they are words of speech that writing does not show, or they are not common. The sources cannot tell which; a speaker can.

## Limits

- **Written text, not speech.** The goal of the course is understanding speech, and no counted collection of spoken Cebuano was found.
- **Small and uneven sources.** Tatoeba is 11,000 words by a few writers; the news is one newspaper; the Bible is another century's language.
- **Rough counting.** Affixes are stripped lightly: some forms are missed, a few credited wrongly. *Ni* "of" and *ni* "this" are counted together, as are *na* "already" and *na* "that". Read a figure to about a tenth of its size.
- **The English matching is one reader's judgment** of 300 words, and each could be argued.
- **The 40 words of no tier were chosen from the commonest unmatched words** and from the English match. The files `data/no-tier-*.tsv` hold more that nobody has looked at.
