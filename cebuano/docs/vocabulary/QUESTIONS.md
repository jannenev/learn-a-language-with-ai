## C. Is there a word here you would not say?

Cross out a word you would not say in everyday talk, and write above it what you say.

- **People:** ako, ko · ikaw, ka · siya · kita, ta · kami, mi · kamo, mo · sila
- **Small words:** ang · si · sa · og · ug · nga · mga · ni · ba · na · pa · lang · gyud, jud · kaayo · pud
- **Yes, no, there is:** oo · dili, di · wala, wa · naa
- **Asking:** unsa · kinsa · asa · pila
- **Meeting people:** maayong buntag · kumusta · salamat · sige · Dong, Day · ayaw
- **Doing:** adto · kaon · inom · tulog · trabaho · gusto · kabalo · kasabot · dali · hulat
- **Things and people:** balay · tubig · pagkaon · kwarta · tawo · Mama, Papa
- **Time:** karon · ugma · unya · buntag · hapon · gabii
- **Describing:** dako · gamay · daghan · lami · maayo
- **Pointing:** kini, ni · kana, na · diri · didto

Do you ever say these yourself? Circle those you do: aduna (for *naa*) · usab (for *pud*) · lamang (for *lang*) · hain (for *asa*) · apan (for *pero*) · alang (for *para*) · mahimo (for *pwede*) · kahibalo (for *kabalo*)
