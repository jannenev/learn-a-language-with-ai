## 3 · Word by word

```
 1  Maayong  buntag,  Ana!
    good     morning
    · maayong = maayo + the linker -ng, which glues a describing word to its noun

 2  Maayong  buntag,  Jun.  Kumusta  ka?
    good     morning        how-are  you

 3  Maayo,  salamat.  Ikaw?
    good    thanks    you
    · ikaw = the full form of "you", used on its own; inside a sentence it is ka

 4  Maayo  pud.  Nikaon  na       ka?
    good   also  ate     already  you
    · kaon = eat; ni- in front = did: nikaon = ate.
    · na = already: the situation has changed. The same na is "now" in a command or plan (Kaon na! = eat now) and "anymore" after a no (Dili na). One word, three English faces; text 003 has all three.
    · "Nikaon na ka?" is the everyday greeting right after hello. Take it as one piece.

 5  Wala  pa.
    not   yet
    · wala pa = not yet, the usual answer; wala = not, for things that did not happen

 6  Naa'y     pagkaon.  Lami!
    there-is  food      tasty
    · naa'y = "there is a ...". pagkaon = food: the root kaon again, with pag- in front

 7  Lami   ba?
    tasty  [?]
    · ba turns the sentence into a yes/no question

 8  Oo,  lami   kaayo.  Gusto  ka?
    yes  tasty  very    want   you
    · kaayo = very; it comes after the word it strengthens

 9  Oo!  Gusto  ko.
    yes  want   I
    · ko = I, ka = you: gusto ko, gusto ka

10  Sige.  Kaon  ta!
    okay   eat   we(let's)
    · ta = we, you and I. Root verb + ta = "let's": Kaon ta!

11  Lami   kaayo!  Salamat,  Ana.
    tasty  very    thanks

12  Ikaw?  Nikaon  na       ka?
    you    ate     already  you

13  Wala  pa.  Gusto  pud   ko  mokaon.
    not   yet  want   also  I   will-eat
    · mo- in front = will: mokaon = will eat. gusto + mo- verb = want to: gusto ko mokaon = I want to eat

14  Sige!  Naa'y     pagkaon.
    okay   there-is  food
```

## Listen for

- **Have you eaten? / Not yet.** *Nikaon na ka?* — *Wala pa.* Lines 4–5 and again 12–13. The pair *na* (already) and *pa* (yet) does the work.
- **One root, four shapes.** *kaon* (eat), *ni-kaon* (ate), *mo-kaon* (will eat), *pag-kaon* (food).
- **I and you.** *Gusto ka?* — *Gusto ko.* Short *ka* = you, short *ko* = I, both right after the first word.
- **Let's …** → root + *ta*: *Kaon ta!*
- **very** → describing word + *kaayo*: *lami kaayo*.
