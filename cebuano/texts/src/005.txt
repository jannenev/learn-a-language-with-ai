title: 005 — Buntag: asa si Jun? (Morning: where is Jun?)
intro: The next morning. Mama has no work today and is at home. Ana gets up; Jun's sleeping mat is empty. Out in the yard, people stand at the water tap with buckets.
tier: Words: Tier 1 only. The last four Tier 1 entries first appear: sila, ug, kasabot, and na as "that" (short for kana; until now na was "already / now"). New shapes of known words: kini and kana, the full forms of ni and na; ta'y, ba'y and kinsa'y, more of the 'y family.
speakers: Ana | Jun | Mama

    ! wala siya diri = he is not here; the opposite is naa siya diri
@ Ana looks out of the window. In the yard, people stand at the water tap with buckets.

Mama | Mga tawo lang. Gusto sila og tubig. | Just people. They want some water.

    ! ug = and. It sounds the same as og (some) in the line before; the place tells them apart: ug joins two things, og comes before the thing wanted.
Mama | Wala. Wala siya didto. | No. He is not there.
    Wala.=not-there Wala=not-there siya=he didto.=there

Ana | Dili ni maayo, Ma. Asa siya? | Not good, this, Ma. Where is he?
    Dili=not ni=this maayo,=good Ma.=Ma Asa=where siya?=he
    ! dili, diri, didto: not, here, there. Three words that look alike; this text has all three.
@ The door opens. Jun comes in, bent sideways under a big bucket. Water spills on the floor.

    ! kana = the full form of na (that). The na in kasabot na ko is the other one: now.

## Listen for

- **They.** *sila*: *Kinsa sila? Gusto sila og tubig. Naa pa sila didto.* With it the persons are complete.
- **And.** *ug*: *Ug si Jun? Tubig ug pagkaon.* It sounds like *og* (some): *gusto sila og tubig* (line 6), *ug si Jun* (line 7).
- **Where is he? / Not here.** *Asa siya?* — *Wala siya diri. Wala siya didto.* The opposite is *naa*: *Naa pa sila didto.*
- **This and that.** *Unsa na?* — *Tubig ni.* (lines 11–12): *na* is that, near you; *ni* is this, near me. The full forms are *kana* and *kini* (lines 14–15).
- **I don't understand.** *Dili ko kasabot.* And when it clears: *Kasabot na ko.*
- **The 'y family grows.** *naa ta'y tubig* (we have water), *naa ba'y pagkaon?* (is there food?), *kinsa'y gusto moinom?* (who wants to drink?).
- **dili, diri, didto.** Not, here, there: lines 4, 8 and 9.
