title: 003 — Gabii sa balay (Evening at home)
intro: Same day as texts 001 and 002, evening. Ana and Jun are at home, the lamp on, waiting for Mama. It is dark when she knocks, back from the market with two bundles of food, one big and one small.
tier: Words: Tier 1 only. Seven entries first appear: siya, mga, kinsa, tulog, ugma, dako, gamay. New shapes of known words: matulog and natulog (ma- and na- on tulog), wala'y, akoa, and ra, the Cebu form of lang.
speakers: Ana | Jun | Mama

@ Evening at home, the lamp on. Ana and Jun sit at the table, waiting for Mama.
Jun | Gabii na, Ana. Asa si Mama? | Night already, Ana. Where is Mama?
    Gabii=night na,=already Ana. Asa=where si=[name] Mama?
    ! gabii na = (it is) night already. gabii, hapon and buntag are the nouns inside maayong gabii, maayong hapon, maayong buntag.
Ana | Sa trabaho pa siya. Daghan ang mga tawo didto. | At work still, she. Many, the people there.
    Sa=at trabaho=work pa=still siya.=she Daghan=many ang=the(subj) mga=[plural] tawo=person didto.=there
    ! siya = he or she; Cebuano has one word for both. Sa trabaho pa siya = she is still at work: the place first, then pa, then siya.
    ! mga (said "manga") makes a noun plural: tawo = person, mga tawo = people.
@ Jun yawns.
Jun | Tulog na ko, Ana. | I'll sleep now, Ana.
    Tulog=sleep na=now ko,=I Ana.
    ! tulog = sleep. Tulog na ko = I'm off to sleep: the bare root with na, as kaon na in text 001. The same ___ na ko frame says hungry now, tired now, here now (line 9).
Ana | Ayaw pa, Dong. Hulat ta. | Don't yet, Dong. Let's wait.
    Ayaw=don't pa,=yet Dong.=to-a-boy Hulat=wait ta.=we(let's)
    ! root + ta = let's, as kaon ta and adto ta: hulat ta = let's wait (for Mama).
@ A knock at the door.
Jun | Ana! Naa'y tawo. Kinsa? | Ana! There is a person. Who?
    Ana! Naa'y=there-is tawo.=person Kinsa?=who
    ! kinsa = who. Naa'y tawo, "there is a person", is how you say someone is there, at the door.
Ana | Wala ko kabalo. Hulat lang diri. | I don't know. Just wait here.
    Wala=not ko=I kabalo.=know Hulat=wait lang=just diri.=here
Mama | Ana! Jun! Naa mo diri? | Ana! Jun! Are you two here?
    Ana! Jun! Naa=is-there mo=you(pl) diri?=here
    ! naa + a place = is here / is there: naa mo diri? = are you (two) here? Mama's voice, from outside the door.
Ana | Si Mama! Dali diri, Ma! | It's Mama! Quick, here, Ma!
    Si=[name] Mama! Dali=quick diri,=here Ma!=Ma
    ! si Mama! = it's Mama: the name with its marker, nothing else. Dali diri! = come here, as in text 002; here it means come in.
@ Mama comes in with two bundles of food, one big, one small, and puts them on the table.
Mama | Naa na ko, Day. Nikaon na mo? | I am here now, Day. Have you eaten already?
    Naa=is-there na=now ko,=I Day.=to-a-girl Nikaon=ate na=already mo?=you(pl)
    ! naa na ko = I'm here now, literally "is-there now I". The same na is "already" in nikaon na mo.
Ana | Wala pa, Ma. Gusto mi mokaon. | Not yet, Ma. We want to eat.
    Wala=not pa,=yet Ma.=Ma Gusto=want mi=we mokaon.=will-eat
    ! gusto + mo- verb = want to: gusto mi mokaon = we want to eat, as gusto ko mokaon in text 001.
Mama | Maayo. Naa ko'y pagkaon. Dako ni. Gamay ni. | Good. I have food. Big, this. Small, this.
    Maayo.=good Naa=there-is ko'y=I pagkaon.=food Dako=big ni.=this Gamay=small ni.=this
    ! dako = big, gamay = small (also "a little, few"). The describing word comes first: dako ni = this is big. ni = this (kini) twice, as Mama holds up one bundle, then the other.
Jun | Dako kaayo! Akoa ang dako! | Very big! Mine, the big one!
    Dako=big kaayo!=very Akoa=mine ang=the(subj) dako!=big
    ! akoa = mine, the pair of imoha = yours (text 002, Imoha na). ang dako = "the big (one)": ang makes a thing out of a describing word. Jun reaches for it.
Mama | Ayaw, Dong. Dili karon. Gamay lang karon. Kaon ta! | Don't, Dong. Not now. Just small now. Let's eat!
    Ayaw,=don't Dong.=to-a-boy Dili=not karon.=now Gamay=small lang=just karon.=now Kaon=eat ta!=we(let's)
    ! dili karon = not now. gamay lang = just a little, just the small one.
Jun | Sige. … Gusto ko moinom. Naa'y tubig, Ana? | OK. … I want to drink. Is there water, Ana?
    Sige.=okay … Gusto=want ko=I moinom.=will-drink Naa'y=there-is tubig,=water Ana?
    ! gusto + mo- verb again: gusto ko moinom = I want to drink. naa'y tubig? = is there water?, as in text 002.
Ana | Oo, naa pa. Daghan pa. | Yes, there-is still. Much still.
    Oo,=yes naa=there-is pa.=still Daghan=much pa.=still
    ! pa = still, and it comes after the word it belongs to: naa pa = there is still (some), daghan pa = much still, there is still a lot.
@ They eat.
Jun | Ma, pila ang mga tawo sa trabaho karon? | Ma, how many were the people at work today?
    Ma,=Ma pila=how-many ang=the(subj) mga=[plural] tawo=person sa=at trabaho=work karon?=today
    ! pila = how many / how much. karon = now, and also "today".
Mama | Daghan gyud kaayo. Ang mga tawo, gusto og pagkaon. | Really very many. The people, want some food.
    Daghan=many gyud=really kaayo.=very Ang=the(subj) mga=[plural] tawo,=person gusto=want og=some pagkaon.=food
    ! Ang mga tawo, gusto og pagkaon: the people are named first, then what they want; the comma is the pause you hear.
Ana | Ma, moadto ka sa trabaho ugma? | Ma, will you go to work tomorrow?
    Ma,=Ma moadto=will-go ka=you sa=to trabaho=work ugma?=tomorrow
    ! ugma = tomorrow. It sits at the end, where karon and unya also sit (text 002: Moadto ba ka unya?).
Mama | Dili. Wala'y trabaho ugma. Naa ra ko diri. | No. There is no work tomorrow. I am just here.
    Dili.=no Wala'y=there-is-no trabaho=work ugma.=tomorrow Naa=is-there ra=just ko=I diri.=here
    ! dili = no / not, for things about now or the future; wala'y = there is no, the pair of naa'y = there is: the squeezed ang glued to wala. Two "no" words with different jobs (grammar-notes, section 5).
    ! ra = lang, the Cebu way of saying "just": naa ra ko diri = I'm just here. Compare hulat lang diri, line 6.
Jun | Maayo kaayo! Ugma, naa ta diri: ikaw, si Ana, ako! | Very good! Tomorrow, we are here: you, Ana, I!
    Maayo=good kaayo!=very Ugma,=tomorrow naa=is-there ta=we diri:=here ikaw,=you si=[name] Ana, ako!=I
    ! ta = we, including the person spoken to; Jun counts them off: you, Ana, me.
Mama | Oo, Dong. … Sige. Matulog na ko. Maayong gabii. | Yes, Dong. … OK. I will sleep now. Good night.
    Oo,=yes Dong.=to-a-boy … Sige.=okay Matulog=will-sleep na=now ko.=I Maayong=good gabii.=night
    ! matulog = will sleep: tulog takes ma- where kaon and adto take mo-. Matulog na ko = I'm going to sleep now; compare tulog na ko, line 3. maayong gabii = good evening, and good night.
Both | Maayong gabii, Ma! | Good night, Ma!
    Maayong=good gabii,=night Ma!=Ma
@ Later. Mama's door is shut. Jun looks at the big bundle.
Jun | Natulog na siya? Gusto pa ko. Ang dako… | Asleep already, she? I want more. The big one…
    Natulog=fell-asleep na=already siya?=she Gusto=want pa=more ko.=I Ang=the(subj) dako…=big
    ! natulog = fell asleep, is asleep: na- is the "did" form of ma- (matulog = will sleep), as ni- is of mo-. gusto pa = want more.
Ana | Oo, natulog na. Ayaw na, Dong. Ugma na lang. Tulog na pud. | Yes, asleep already. Don't anymore, Dong. Tomorrow instead. Sleep now too.
    Oo,=yes natulog=fell-asleep na.=already Ayaw=don't na,=anymore Dong.=to-a-boy Ugma=tomorrow na=already lang.=just Tulog=sleep na=now pud.=also
    ! ugma na lang, literally "tomorrow already just", is the set phrase for "tomorrow instead", "leave it for tomorrow". tulog na = sleep now, the bare root as a command, as kaon na. Four na in one line: already, anymore, instead, now.
Jun | Sige. Maayong gabii, Ana. | OK. Good night, Ana.
    Sige.=okay Maayong=good gabii,=night Ana.

## Listen for

- **He, she.** *siya* is he and she at once: *Sa trabaho pa siya. Natulog na siya.* No gender, and no "it" either.
- **Plural with mga.** *mga tawo* = people, said "manga": *Daghan ang mga tawo. Pila ang mga tawo?*
- **One root, three shapes.** *tulog* (sleep): *Tulog na ko* (the bare root: I'm off to sleep), *matulog na ko* (ma- = will), *natulog na siya* (na- = did, is asleep). Verbs of state take *ma-* / *na-* where *kaon* and *adto* take *mo-* / *ni-*.
- **Two "no" words.** *Dili* for now and the future and as the plain answer (*Dili. Dili karon.*); *wala* for what is not there and what did not happen (*Wala ko kabalo. Wala pa. Wala'y trabaho ugma.*).
- **The 'y family.** *naa'y, wala'y, ko'y*: the squeezed *ang* glued to the word before it.
- **lang and ra.** The same word: *hulat lang diri* (line 6), *naa ra ko diri* (line 19). Cebu says *ra* as often as *lang*.
- **The describing word first.** *Dako ni. Gamay ni. Dako kaayo. Daghan pa. Natulog na siya.* The quality or the verb first, then the thing or the particle.
- **ni and na, again.** *dako ni, gamay ni* (this); *gabii na* (already), *ayaw na* (anymore), *tulog na* (now), *ugma na lang* (instead).
