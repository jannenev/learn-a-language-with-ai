# 004 — Gabii sa balay (Evening at home)

Same day as texts 001 to 003, evening. Ana and Jun are at home, the lamp on, waiting for Mama. It is dark when she knocks, back from the market with two bundles of food, one big and one small.

Words: Tier 1 only. Seven entries first appear: siya, mga, kinsa, tulog, ugma, dako, gamay. New shapes of known words: matulog and natulog (ma- and na- on tulog), wala'y, akoa, and ra, the Cebu form of lang.

How to use: read block 1 on its own first. Then block 2 to check what you understood. Block 3 only for the words you could not place. The English in block 2 stays close to the Cebuano words, so it is not always natural English; where the real meaning differs, block 3 says so.

## 3 · Word by word

```
 1  Gabii  na,      Ana.  Asa    si      Mama?
    night  already        where  [name]
    · gabii na = (it is) night already. gabii, hapon and buntag are the nouns inside maayong gabii, maayong hapon, maayong buntag.

 2  Sa  trabaho  pa     siya.  Daghan  ang        mga       tawo    didto.
    at  work     still  she    many    the(subj)  [plural]  person  there
    · siya = he or she; Cebuano has one word for both. Sa trabaho pa siya = she is still at work: the place first, then pa, then siya.
    · mga (said "manga") makes a noun plural: tawo = person, mga tawo = people.

 3  Tulog  na   ko,  Ana.
    sleep  now  I
    · tulog = sleep. Tulog na ko = I'm off to sleep: the bare root with na, as kaon na in text 001. The same ___ na ko frame says hungry now, tired now, here now (line 9).

 4  Ayaw   pa,  Dong.     Hulat  ta.
    don't  yet  to-a-boy  wait   we(let's)
    · root + ta = let's, as kaon ta and adto ta: hulat ta = let's wait (for Mama).

 5  Ana!  Naa'y     tawo.   Kinsa?
          there-is  person  who
    · kinsa = who. Naa'y tawo, "there is a person", is how you say someone is there, at the door.

 6  Wala  ko  kabalo.  Hulat  lang  diri.
    not   I   know     wait   just  here

 7  Ana!  Jun!  Naa       mo       diri?
                is-there  you(pl)  here
    · naa + a place = is here / is there: naa mo diri? = are you (two) here? Mama's voice, from outside the door.

 8  Si      Mama!  Dali   diri,  Ma!
    [name]         quick  here   Ma
    · si Mama! = it's Mama: the name with its marker, nothing else. Dali diri! = come here, as in text 003; here it means come in.

 9  Naa       na   ko,  Day.       Nikaon  na       mo?
    is-there  now  I    to-a-girl  ate     already  you(pl)
    · naa na ko = I'm here now, literally "is-there now I". The same na is "already" in nikaon na mo.

10  Wala  pa,  Ma.  Gusto  mi  mokaon.
    not   yet  Ma   want   we  will-eat
    · gusto + mo- verb = want to: gusto mi mokaon = we want to eat, as gusto pud ko mokaon in text 001.

11  Maayo.  Naa       ko'y  pagkaon.  Dako  ni.   Gamay  ni.
    good    there-is  I     food      big   this  small  this
    · dako = big, gamay = small (also "a little, few"). The describing word comes first: dako ni = this is big. ni = this (kini) twice, as Mama holds up one bundle, then the other.

12  Dako  kaayo!  Akoa  ang        dako!
    big   very    mine  the(subj)  big
    · akoa = mine, the pair of imoha = yours (text 003, Imoha na). ang dako = "the big (one)": ang makes a thing out of a describing word. Jun reaches for it.

13  Ayaw,  Dong.     Dili  karon.  Gamay  lang  karon.  Kaon  ta!
    don't  to-a-boy  not   now     small  just  now     eat   we(let's)
    · dili karon = not now. gamay lang = just a little, just the small one.

14  Sige.  …  Gusto  ko  moinom.     Naa'y     tubig,  Ana?
    okay      want   I   will-drink  there-is  water
    · gusto + mo- verb again: gusto ko moinom = I want to drink. naa'y tubig? = is there water?, as in text 003.

15  Oo,  naa       pa.    Daghan  pa.
    yes  there-is  still  much    still
    · pa = still, and it comes after the word it belongs to: naa pa = there is still (some), daghan pa = much still, there is still a lot.

16  Ma,  pila      ang        mga       tawo    sa  trabaho  karon?
    Ma   how-many  the(subj)  [plural]  person  at  work     today
    · pila = how many / how much. karon = now, and also "today".

17  Daghan  gyud    kaayo.  Ang        mga       tawo,   gusto  og    pagkaon.
    many    really  very    the(subj)  [plural]  person  want   some  food
    · Ang mga tawo, gusto og pagkaon: the people are named first, then what they want; the comma is the pause you hear.

18  Ma,  moadto   ka   sa  trabaho  ugma?
    Ma   will-go  you  to  work     tomorrow
    · ugma = tomorrow. It sits at the end, where karon and unya also sit (text 003: Moadto ba ka unya?).

19  Dili.  Wala'y       trabaho  ugma.     Naa       ra    ko  diri.
    no     there-is-no  work     tomorrow  is-there  just  I   here
    · dili = no / not, for things about now or the future; wala'y = there is no, the pair of naa'y = there is: the squeezed ang glued to wala. Two "no" words with different jobs (grammar-notes, section 5).
    · ra = lang, the Cebu way of saying "just": naa ra ko diri = I'm just here. Compare hulat lang diri, line 6.

20  Maayo  kaayo!  Ugma,     naa       ta  diri:  ikaw,  si      Ana,  ako!
    good   very    tomorrow  is-there  we  here   you    [name]        I
    · ta = we, including the person spoken to; Jun counts them off: you, Ana, me.

21  Oo,  Dong.     …  Sige.  Matulog     na   ko.  Maayong  gabii.
    yes  to-a-boy     okay   will-sleep  now  I    good     night
    · matulog = will sleep: tulog takes ma- where kaon and adto take mo-. Matulog na ko = I'm going to sleep now; compare tulog na ko, line 3. maayong gabii = good evening, and good night.

22  Maayong  gabii,  Ma!
    good     night   Ma

23  Natulog      na       siya?  Gusto  pa    ko.  Ang        dako…
    fell-asleep  already  she    want   more  I    the(subj)  big
    · natulog = fell asleep, is asleep: na- is the "did" form of ma- (matulog = will sleep), as ni- is of mo-. gusto pa = want more.

24  Oo,  natulog      na.      Ayaw   na,      Dong.     Ugma      na       lang.  Tulog  na   pud.
    yes  fell-asleep  already  don't  anymore  to-a-boy  tomorrow  already  just   sleep  now  also
    · ugma na lang, literally "tomorrow already just", is the set phrase for "tomorrow instead", "leave it for tomorrow". tulog na = sleep now, the bare root as a command, as kaon na. Four na in one line: already, anymore, instead, now.

25  Sige.  Maayong  gabii,  Ana.
    okay   good     night
```

## Listen for

- **He, she.** *siya* is he and she at once: *Sa trabaho pa siya. Natulog na siya.* No gender, and no "it" either.
- **Plural with mga.** *mga tawo* = people, said "manga": *Daghan ang mga tawo. Pila ang mga tawo?*
- **One root, three shapes.** *tulog* (sleep): *Tulog na ko* (the bare root: I'm off to sleep), *matulog na ko* (ma- = will), *natulog na siya* (na- = did, is asleep). Verbs of state take *ma-* / *na-* where *kaon* and *adto* take *mo-* / *ni-*.
- **Two "no" words.** *Dili* for now and the future and as the plain answer (*Dili. Dili karon.*); *wala* for what is not there and what did not happen (*Wala ko kabalo. Wala pa. Wala'y trabaho ugma.*).
- **The 'y family.** *naa'y, wala'y, ko'y*: the squeezed *ang* glued to the word before it.
- **lang and ra.** The same word: *hulat lang diri* (line 6), *naa ra ko diri* (line 19). Cebu says *ra* as often as *lang*.
- **The describing word first.** *Dako ni. Gamay ni. Dako kaayo. Daghan pa. Natulog na siya.* The quality or the verb first, then the thing or the particle.
- **ni and na, again.** *dako ni, gamay ni* (this); *gabii na* (already), *ayaw na* (anymore), *tulog na* (now), *ugma na lang* (instead).
