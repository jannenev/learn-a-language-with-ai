## 3 · Word by word

```
 1  Lami   ni,   Dong.
    tasty  this  to-a-boy
    · Dong = how you address a boy or a younger brother (short for Dodong)
    · ni = this (short for kini); it comes last: lami ni = this is tasty

 2  Unsa  ni?
    what  this

 3  Wala  ko  kabalo.  Kaon  lang.
    not   I   know     eat   just
    · wala ko kabalo, literally "not I know", is the everyday "I don't know"
    · lang = just, only; it comes after the word it belongs to

 4  Lami   gyud    ni!   Daghan  kaayo!
    tasty  really  this  much    very
    · gyud = really, for sure; it comes after the word it strengthens, like kaayo

 5  Oo,  daghan  gyud.   Daghan  ang        pagkaon.
    yes  much    really  much    the(subj)  food
    · ang marks the subject, what the sentence is about; "the food is much" = there's a lot of food

 6  Nikaon  na       si      Mama?
    ate     already  [name]
    · si marks a person's name as the subject; it has no English equivalent

 7  Oo.
    yes

 8  Asa    si      Mama?  Naa       sa  balay?
    where  [name]         is-there  in  house

 9  Wala       sa  balay.  Naa       sa  trabaho.
    not-there  in  house   is-there  at  work
    · no "she": the subject is left out when it is obvious from the question
    · sa = in, at, to: the one word for place. wala = is not there, naa = is there; the same words as wala (not) and naa'y (there is) in text 001

10  Sa  trabaho?  Adto  ta!
    at  work      go    we(let's)
    · root verb + ta = "let's": Adto ta! like Kaon ta!

11  Karon?
    now

12  Oo,  karon!
    yes  now

13  Unya   lang.  Kaon  ta         karon.  Adto  ta         unya.
    later  just   eat   we(let's)  now     go    we(let's)  later

14  Moadto   ko  didto  unya.
    will-go  I   there  later
    · mo- = will, as in mokaon in text 001: moadto = will go. didto = there, far from both of us

15  Moadto   pud   ko  didto.  Naa       ko'y  kwarta.
    will-go  also  I   there   there-is  I     money
    · naa ko'y kwarta = literally "there is, I, money" = I have money

16  Naa       pud   ko'y  kwarta.  Sige,  Dong.
    there-is  also  I     money    okay   to-a-boy
```

## Listen for

- **What's this? / I don't know.** *Unsa ni?* — *Wala ko kabalo.*
- **Where is … ? / Is at …** *Asa si Mama?* — *Wala sa balay. Naa sa trabaho.* The pair *wala* (not there) and *naa* (there), and *sa* for the place.
- **Now and later.** *Kaon ta karon. Adto ta unya.* One-word answers work too: *Karon?* — *Unya.*
- **Let's go / I'll go.** *Adto ta!* is built like *Kaon ta!* in text 001; *mo-* in front makes it "will": *Moadto ko*.
- **There is / I have.** *Naa ko'y kwarta* (I have money), built like *Naa'y pagkaon* in text 001.
